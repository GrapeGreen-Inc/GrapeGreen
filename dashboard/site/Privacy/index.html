🍇 GrapeGreen Privacy

Welcome to GrapeGreen! We take your privacy and data security incredibly seriously. Below is exactly how we keep your information safe and secure.

🛡️ Multi-Layer Security

Your account credentials and chat messages aren't just sitting in a regular file. We secure your data using advanced multi-layered backend systems, combining robust encoding methods with full AES-256 encryption. Nobody can read your private messages or data.

⚠️ Account Responsibility & Compromise

While we work around the clock to protect our servers, GrapeGreen and its administration team are not responsible or liable for any account compromise, hacking, data leaks, or unauthorized entry resulting from weak passwords, shared login details, or external security breaches on the user's side. You are entirely responsible for keeping your temporary and permanent passwords safe.

👶 Under 13 Privacy Rules

We are fully committed to protecting our younger users. If you are under 13:

  • We do not store your email address.
  • We do not store your full date of birth (only your birth year is kept).
  • You get access to a dedicated chatroom fully moderated by our administration team.

📊 What We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum data needed to run your account:

  • Your chosen username.
  • Your password (fully encrypted).
  • Your dashboard theme preferences.
